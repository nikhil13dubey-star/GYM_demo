Frequently Asked Questions

+
1. What is this? ▾

A demo of a partner-gym membership portal. Search for a gym near you, compare plans and register your interest. The data shown here is sample data.

2. How do I find a gym near me? ▾

Enter a 6-digit PIN code for an exact match, or type a city or area name. Results are sorted by distance from the location you searched.

3. What happens after I submit interest? ▾

Your enquiry is recorded as a lead, assigned automatically to an available agent, and you receive a confirmation email. An agent then contacts you to complete the membership.

4. Is any payment taken on this site? ▾

No. Payment is handled outside this application; the portal only records the payment reference once it has been received.
